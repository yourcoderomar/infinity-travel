# Infinity Vibes

Travel · Events · Experiences. Group trips out of Cairo to Dahab, Fayoum, Ain El Sokhna and beyond, sold mostly through Instagram and WhatsApp. The brand is bold and energetic: lime on blue, heavy uppercase titles, and the four-point spark.

This system follows the official **Infinity Vibes Brand Identity 2026** guidelines (the PDF in `assets/Guidelines`). Where earlier posts differ from the guidelines, the guidelines win.

## Voice

- Short and energetic, never corporate. Destination names do the shouting: **DAHAB**, **FAYOUM**, **AIN EL SOKHNA**.
- Facts are plain and scannable: trip length (`4 Days/3 Nights`), dates (`15/10` or `9 october`), and a clear next step (`Book by request`).
- One idea per post: a place, its dates, how to book.
- Write in English; Egyptian Arabic in captions is welcome. Neither brand font has Arabic glyphs, so Arabic falls back to the system face.

## Colour

Five brand colours, exactly as the guidelines specify. Don't add others.

| Token | HEX | CMYK | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `blue` | #4949EA | 78 73 0 0 | the badge, big fields, actions |
| `lime` | #DDF444 | 18 0 85 0 | INFINITY, the spark, highlights |
| `white` | #FFFFFF | 0 0 0 0 | VIBES, page ground |
| `light-grey` | #E6E7E8 | 0 0 0 10 | quiet panels, dividers |
| `black` | #000000 | 0 0 0 100 | text, black-and-white logo |

| Pairing | Contrast |
| --- | --- |
| white on blue | 6.1:1 |
| lime on blue | 5.0:1 |
| black on lime (`on-highlight`) | 17:1 |
| black on white / light grey | 21:1 / 16:1 |

- Never lime or white text on white / light grey; never black text on blue (3.4:1).
- Older trip posts used other blues (#004AAD), a navy and a sun yellow. They are not brand colours; new work uses the five above.

## Typography

- **Galano Grotesque** for titles, all weights. Heavy (800) uppercase for destination names and section titles. Section titles can pair a regular first line with a heavy second line, as in the guidelines (THE LOGO / **ANATOMY**).
- **Poppins** for titles and body text, all weights. Everything that isn't a big title: dates, details, buttons, captions.
- Galano Grotesque is a licensed font and its files aren't in this system yet, so titles currently fall back to Poppins ExtraBold. Add the licensed `.woff2` files to switch it on.
- Captions and footers: Poppins, uppercase, wide tracking (`caption`), like "BRAND IDENTITY 2026".

## Shape and layout

- Rounded and friendly, echoing the circular badge: `radius-lg` on cards and photo panels, `radius-pill` on dates and buttons, `radius-sm` only for small rectangular tags.
- Flat colour, no drop shadows or glows anywhere (the logo forbids them; the system follows suit).
- Instagram posts are 1080 × 1440 (4:5): full-bleed trip photo, heavy uppercase destination name in the top third, dates in lime pills or a blue date card, `space-12` safe margin.

## Iconography and motifs

- **The spark**: the four-point star from the logo, in lime. Large and cropped as a background shape (the guidelines' cover), or small as a single accent.
- **Hand-drawn white doodles** on posts: shell, sun, snorkel mask, jeep, an arrow to the dates. One or two per post, white only.
- No emoji in designed pieces.

## Logo

The logo is the **circular badge**: INFINITY in lime and VIBES in white, set on a tilt with the spark and orbit swoosh, on a blue disc. See `assets/Logos`.

- **Main execution** (blue badge) works on all backgrounds.
- **Black and white executions** (solid black badge, white badge with black ring) are for limited use only: one-colour print, stamps, embossing.
- **Clear space**: keep at least the height of the "S" of VIBES clear on every side.
- **Don't**: stretch or squeeze it, recolour it with non-brand colours, change its letter spacing, add shadows, or add glows.
